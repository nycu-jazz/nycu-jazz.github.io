Who Says There’s No Jazz at NYCU?

Professor 林明學 (opens in a new tab) of the Institute of Music first offered Jazz Harmony, Improvisation and Ensemble in spring 2020. In Room EE115 of Engineering Building V, students learn to swing, work with jazz harmony, and improvise together on their own instruments. Later in the semester, they choose jazz standards in small groups and prepare to perform.

Students in the course built and maintain this site. For more information, see the course syllabus (opens in a new tab).
